Why the contours bend

Reach is computed by OpenRouteService, which routes over the live OpenStreetMap network and returns the area you can actually reach within each time band — not a circle drawn from average speed.

Walking is modelled at roughly 5 km/h. Cycling speed depends on the way: about 18 km/h on cycleways and quiet residential streets, slower on rough paths, faster downhill.

Watch what the Mississippi does to the contours: because crossings funnel onto a handful of bridges, the bands pinch hard at the river and bulge along grade-separated trails like the Midtown Greenway, the city's car-free "bike highway."

Each band lists the cumulative reachable area, and the resident population inside it where OpenRouteService can estimate it.
